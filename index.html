Faro · Earthquake information and local help

Created by Oktsec. Earthquakes from USGS and IGC, local help and preparedness for people away from home.

The app checks the catalogs every 60 seconds while it is open. Records can be published with a delay; it is not an early warning system.

Available in English and Spanish. Your personal data stays in this browser; sharing a message always requires your action.
