# Community conduct

Treat contributors and people affected by disasters with respect. Welcome different languages, experiences and abilities. Discuss proposals and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats or unwanted disclosure of personal information.

Use synthetic examples. Do not post another person's location, medical situation, contact details or emergency messages. Do not use this project to coordinate emergency response; contact the appropriate local services.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. They should explain decisions when appropriate, protect reporter privacy and handle appeals impartially. Ask maintainers for a private channel before reporting sensitive incidents; do not include incident details in a public request. Initial maintainer: [@garagon](https://github.com/garagon).
